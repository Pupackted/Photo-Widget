# Framed: Photo Widgets — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 2026_

Framed puts photos you choose on your Home Screen. It is built so that nothing you add ever leaves your device.

## Your photos stay on your device
Photos you choose are copied into the app's private storage on your device so your widgets can show them. They are never uploaded, shared or sent anywhere.

## Only the photos you choose
Framed normally uses the system photo picker, so it only sees the photos you select.

## Albums and On This Day
If you sync a widget with an album or turn on On This Day, Framed asks for access to your photo library. It reads the library only on your device, only to choose photos for those widgets. Nothing is copied off the device. You can change this at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Photos.

## No accounts, no tracking
There's no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party code. The app makes no network connections and collects no data.

## Backups
If you back up your device to iCloud or a computer, your widgets are included in that backup like any other app data.

## Deleting your data
Deleting a widget deletes its photos from the app. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

## Children
Framed does not collect any information from anyone, including children.
